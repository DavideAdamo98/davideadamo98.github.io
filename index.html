Davide Adamo

CEPAM · Université Côte d’Azur · Inria

Hello! I'm an applied mathematician with interests in the foundations of mathematics and machine learning. Since November 2023, I am a PhD candidate in applied mathematics at the UMR7264 CEPAM laboratory and Inria Sophia Antipolis in the Maasai team, superived by Marco Corneli, Emmanuelle Vila and Manon Vuillien.

My work focuses on the development and application of machine learning models for 3D scan of archaeozoological bones.

My research interests include:

  • Topological data analysis
  • Kernel Methods for classification
  • Computational Optimal Transport : geometric matching and barycentric learning
