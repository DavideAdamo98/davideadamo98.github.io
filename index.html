Davide Adamo

CEPAM · Université Côte d’Azur · Inria

Hello! I'm an applied mathematician with interests in the foundations of mathematics and machine learning. Since November 2023, I have been a PhD student at the UMR7264 CEPAM laboratory and Inria Sophia Antipolis in the Maasai team, superived by Marco Corneli, Emmanuelle Vila and Manon Vuillien.

My work focuses on the development and application of machine/deep learning models on archaeozoological data. Starting from 3D scans of modern and archaeological bones, I became interested in the use of topological descriptors for the supervised classification of animal species. I also contribute to the development of new optimal transport techniques for rigid matching and barycentric learning.
